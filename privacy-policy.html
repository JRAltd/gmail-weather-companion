Privacy Policy — Gmail Weather Companion

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Gmail Weather Companion (the "Extension") is a browser extension that displays live weather, an interactive radar map, and packing tips for a location you choose, in a sidebar docked inside Gmail. This policy explains what data the Extension accesses, how it is used, and what is not done with it.

What Data the Extension Accesses

  • Searched or saved location: The city/zip you search, or the default location, is used to request weather and radar data.
  • Email subject/body text (local matching only): When you open an email in Gmail, the Extension reads the visible subject and body text locally in your browser to check whether it mentions one of a small built-in list of city names. This comparison happens entirely on your device — the email text itself is never sent anywhere.
  • No account credentials: The Extension does not access, store, or transmit your Gmail password, authentication tokens, or account credentials. There is no sign-in of any kind.

How Data Is Used

  • Your selected location (city/zip name or coordinates) is sent to Open-Meteo to fetch current and forecast weather, and to RainViewer/CartoDB to render the radar map tiles.
  • Email text is only used locally to detect a mention of a known city name and is never transmitted to any server, including Open-Meteo, RainViewer, or CartoDB.

Data Storage and Retention

  • Your saved location and temperature unit preference are stored in your browser via chrome.storage.sync and are cleared if you remove the Extension.
  • The Extension does not maintain a remote database of user emails, locations, or personal information.

Data Sharing

  • The Extension does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • The only external communication is to Open-Meteo (weather/geocoding), RainViewer (radar tiles), and CartoDB (map tiles) — no email content, sender/recipient information, or account identifiers are included in those requests.

Your Choices

  • You can disable or uninstall the Extension at any time through your browser's extension manager.
  • The Extension only operates on Gmail pages you have open — it does not run in the background when Gmail is not open in an active tab.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact: For questions about this privacy policy or the Extension's data practices, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
