Privacy Policy for Gmail Weather Companion

Last Updated: August 2026

Gmail Weather Companion ("we", "our") values your privacy. This privacy policy describes how our Chrome Extension handles information when you use our extension inside Google Chrome.

1. Information We Access

The Extension uses the city/zip you search (or a default location) to request weather and radar data. When you open an email in mail.google.com, our content script also checks the visible subject and body text locally in your browser against a small built-in list of city names — this check happens entirely on your device, and the email text is never sent to us or anyone else.

2. How We Use Information

Your selected location is sent via HTTPS to Open-Meteo (weather and geocoding) and to RainViewer/CartoDB (radar and map tiles) to display the forecast and radar map. We do NOT collect, save, transmit, or sell personal identifiers, email account data, contact lists, message history, or email content — there is no account sign-in and no backend server of ours involved.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

We do not store your emails or personal information on external servers or databases. Settings such as your saved location and temperature preference (°C/°F) are stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.sync.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact our support team.
