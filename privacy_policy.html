Privacy Policy for Gmail Weather Companion

Last Updated: August 2026

Gmail Weather Companion ("we", "our") values your privacy. This privacy policy describes how our Chrome Extension handles information when you use our extension inside Google Chrome.

1. Information We Access

When you open an email in mail.google.com, our content script scans the active email subject and message body locally in your browser strictly to extract geographical location references (such as cities, airport codes, or landmark destinations).

2. How We Use Information

Extracted location query strings are sent via HTTPS to our server endpoint to retrieve weather data and generate weather summaries via Gemini AI. We do NOT collect, save, transmit, or sell personal identifiers, email account data, contact lists, or message history.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

We do not store your emails or personal information on external servers or databases. Settings such as temperature preferences (°C/°F) are stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.sync.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact our support team.
